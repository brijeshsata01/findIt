
# CHAPTER 3 – REVIEW OF LITERATURE

## 3.1 Study of Existing Similar Systems

### 1. Manual Lost and Found System

In a manual lost and found system, students
report lost items directly to the college
administration or security office.

The staff may maintain records of lost and
found items manually.

Limitations:
- Requires manual communication.
- Searching records may take time.
- Students may need to visit the office.
- Item information may not be centrally
  accessible to all students.

### 2. Social Media and Messaging Platforms

Students may use messaging groups and social
media platforms to share information about
lost and found belongings.

Limitations:
- Messages can get buried among other posts.
- Searching old information can be difficult.
- Item records may not be organized.
- Claim verification may require manual
  communication.

### 3. General Online Lost and Found Platforms

Online lost and found platforms allow users
to publish information about lost and found
belongings through websites or applications.

Limitations to investigate:
- Some platforms may not provide features
  designed specifically for a university.
- Campus-specific administration may not be
  available.
- Their claim verification process may differ.
- Their functionality may vary by platform.



## 3.2 Comparative Analysis of Existing
Systems and Proposed Solution

The existing approaches for managing lost
and found items have different limitations.

Manual systems depend on direct communication
with college staff. Messaging platforms
provide quick communication but may not
organize item records effectively.

General online platforms provide digital
reporting and searching facilities, but their
features and suitability for a specific campus
need to be evaluated.

The proposed FindIt system aims to provide
a centralized campus-focused platform with
the following features:

- Separate Student and Admin modules.
- Structured Lost and Found reports.
- Photograph upload and viewing.
- Search and filtering functionality.
- Claim request management.
- Admin verification and status updates.

The proposed solution focuses on organizing
the lost and found process within a university
campus.


## 3.3 Identification of Research or
Functional Gaps and Scope for Innovation

Based on the preliminary study of existing
approaches, the following functional gaps
have been identified for further evaluation:

1. Lack of a centralized campus-focused
   platform for lost and found records.

2. Difficulty in organizing item details
   shared through informal communication.

3. Need for structured photograph-based
   item reporting.

4. Need for a systematic claim request
   and verification process.

5. Need for separate access and management
   facilities for students and administrators.

6. Need for item status tracking and
   organized record management.

### Scope for Innovation

The proposed FindIt system aims to address
these requirements through:

- Campus-specific Lost and Found Portal.
- Separate Student and Admin Dashboards.
- Photograph-based Item Reporting.
- Search and Filter Features.
- Claim Request and Verification Workflow.
- Item Status Management.

These features will be implemented and
evaluated as part of the proposed system.
